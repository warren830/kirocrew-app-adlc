# Software Requests and Licensing

## Catalog software

Software listed in the IT catalog installs from the company app store without approval. Licenses are assigned automatically and reclaimed after 90 days of inactivity.

## Non-catalog software

- Software outside the catalog requires approval from your manager and from the security team.
- Requests are raised as P4 tickets with the business justification and vendor name.
- Local administrator rights are not granted to end users; installations are performed by the endpoint tooling.

## Prohibited software

Unlicensed software, personal cloud-sync clients not on the approved list, and tools that disable endpoint protection are prohibited.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q: Where is the lost-and-found?**
A: Lost items are kept at reception for 30 days.

**Q: Can I bring my dog to the office?**
A: Pets are not permitted in laboratory areas; office areas follow the site policy.
