# VPN Access Policy

## Scope

This policy describes how Northwind Robotics employees connect to internal systems from outside the office network.

## Requirements

- VPN is available only from a company-managed device enrolled in device management.
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for every VPN session.
- Personal devices (including personal tablets and phones) are not permitted on the VPN.
- Contractors receive time-boxed VPN accounts that expire with their contract.

## Self-service credential reset

- Employees may reset their own VPN credentials once every 24 hours through the IT portal (Self-service > VPN > Reset).
- New credentials are delivered to the corporate mailbox only.
- A second reset inside the same 24-hour window requires a helpdesk ticket and identity verification.

## Troubleshooting order

1. Confirm the device is enrolled and up to date.
2. Confirm MFA prompts arrive on the registered authenticator.
3. Reset VPN credentials through self-service.
4. If the connection still fails, open a ticket; connection failures that stop you from working are Priority 2.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q: Where can I find the cafeteria menu for this week?**
A: The menu is posted on the facilities intranet page every Monday.

**Q: How do I book a parking space at the Shenzhen campus?**
A: Use the facilities app; spaces are released at 07:00 each day.

**Q: Is the printer on floor 3 out of toner?**
A: Facilities replaces toner on request through the office-services form.

**Q: Is there a recorded VPN session for new joiners in the training calendar?**
A: The onboarding recording is listed in the learning portal under Getting connected.

**Q: Can I book the quiet room for a VPN session with a supplier?**
A: Quiet rooms are booked through the facilities app like any meeting room.
