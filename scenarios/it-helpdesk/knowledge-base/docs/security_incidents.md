# Reporting Security Incidents

## What counts as an incident

Suspected phishing, a compromised or shared password, a lost device, malware alerts, or any unexpected MFA prompt.

## Immediate actions

1. Do not forward the suspicious email to colleagues.
2. Report it with the 'Report phishing' button or to the security mailbox listed on the IT portal.
3. If you entered credentials, change your password immediately through self-service and tell the security team.
4. The security team acknowledges reports within 15 minutes during business hours.

## What the helpdesk and assistants will never do

They will never ask you to share a password or MFA code, never tell you to ignore a suspicious prompt, and never close a security report without the security team's confirmation.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q: How do I submit a holiday request?**
A: Holiday requests are submitted in the people system and approved by your manager.

**Q: Who do I ask about my payslip?**
A: Payslip questions go to the payroll mailbox, not to the IT helpdesk.
