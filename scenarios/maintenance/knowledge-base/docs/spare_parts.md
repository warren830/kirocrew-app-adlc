# Spare Parts and Critical Stock

## Critical spares

Critical spares are parts whose absence stops a line for more than 4 hours. They carry a minimum stock level in the plant store and are reordered automatically when stock falls to the minimum.

## Requesting parts

Parts are requested against a work order. The store issues parts to technicians with a valid work order number; loose issue without a work order is not permitted.

## Lead times

- Standard stocked parts: same shift.
- Non-stocked supplier parts: 5 working days.
- Custom machined parts: 15 working days via the approved workshop.

## Substitutes

A substitute part may be fitted only if it appears on the approved-equivalents list for that equipment. Food-contact parts must carry a food-grade certificate; no exceptions.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q: Does the plant shuttle run on public holidays?**
A: The shuttle runs a reduced timetable on public holidays; check the notice board.

**Q: How do I get a visitor badge for a contractor?**
A: Contractor badges are issued by security with a signed induction form.

**Q: Who keeps the key to the cap steriliser viewing cabinet?**
A: The shift lead keeps cabinet keys in the key safe at the line office.

**Q: Can we stack empty pallets next to the cap steriliser?**
A: No. Empty pallets go to the marked zone behind warehouse B.
